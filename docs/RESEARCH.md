## Patterns

All five do this:
- You move. Weapons fire on their own.
- Pick 1 of 3–4 upgrades each level.
- Players get tools to fight the RNG.
- Tags or sets reward stacking a theme.
- Boss beats land at fixed points.
- New characters unlock through goals.

Players praise:
- Short runs that pull you into one more.
- Characters that play differently.
- Finding a build that breaks the game.
- Low price for the hours.
- Runs on Steam Deck and low-end PCs.

Players punish:
- Slowdown with big hordes. Four of five games get this complaint.
- Your own effects hiding enemy attacks.
- Grindy permanent upgrades.
- Runs over 20 minutes.
- Crashes that wipe a run.
- Side content that feels like filler.
