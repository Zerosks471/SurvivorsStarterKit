Wave 3The patterns

All five do this

  • Move only. Weapons fire on their own.
  • Pick 1 of 3–4 upgrades on each level.
  • Give players a way to fight the RNG.
  • Tags or sets that reward stacking a theme.
  • Boss beats at fixed points in the run.
  • Unlock new characters through goals.

What players praise

  • "One more run" pull from short runs.
  • Characters that play differently, not just stat swaps.
  • Finding a build that breaks the game.
  • Low price for the hours played.
  • Runs on Steam Deck and low-end PCs.

What players punish

  • Slowdown with big hordes. Four of five games get this complaint.
  • Your own effects hiding enemy attacks.
  • Grindy permanent upgrades.
  • Runs over 20 minutes.
  • Crashes that wipe a run.
  • Side content that feels like filler.

Wave 6Concept: Echo Crypt

Working title

The crypt learns how you fight. Your rites fire when you act. When you fall, you become the crypt's next warden.

Setting
A living crypt full of skeletons. The starter kit already ships KayKit dungeon and skeleton art.
You play
A warden-hunter who fights with rites, spells bound to actions.
Run
15 minutes. A boss every 3 minutes. Your Echo shows up at 9:00.
Feel
Dark loot, closest to Halls of Torment. The kit's art points this way.
View
3D, top-down camera, from the kit.
Stack
Godot 4.6 .NET, C#, Jolt physics.

Wave 7Signature features

The three features that set Echo Crypt apart. None of the five games you researched do these.

Signature 1

The Crypt Learns

The game tracks your damage by tag. Each level-up, the crypt gains resistance to your top tag, up to 50%. You have to branch out or push through.

Kit head start: every upgrade card already pairs a player buff with an enemy buff. You swap the random enemy pick for a counter-pick.

// On level-up, counter the player's top tag
var top = _damageByTag.MaxBy(kv => kv.Value).Key;
_resist[top] = Mathf.Min(_resist[top] + 0.15f, 0.5f);
// Card shows: "You: Fire Rite +1 | Crypt: Fire resist 30%"
Signature 2

Rites

Each rite has a trigger, an effect, and a tag. A Bone Nova fires on dash. A Blood Ring fires when you get hit. A Frost Lance fires after you stand still for 2 seconds.

Kit head start: the 4 existing spells (shooting, lifesteal, floating sphere, spirit water) become the first 4 effects.

public enum RiteTrigger { Timer, Dash, Hurt, StandStill, KillStreak }
public enum RiteTag { Bone, Blood, Frost, Spirit, Fire }

[GlobalClass]
public partial class RiteDefinition : Resource {
  [Export] public RiteTrigger Trigger;
  [Export] public float TriggerValue;   // seconds or kills
  [Export] public RiteTag Tag;
  [Export] public PackedScene Effect;   // bolt, nova, orbit, zone
  [Export] public float[] DamageByLevel;
}
Signature 3

Your Echo

When you die, the game saves your build. Next run, at 9:00, your Echo spawns as a boss using your rites against you. Kill it and you keep one of its rites to start your next run.

Kit head start: reuse the boss prefab. Attach your saved rites with the enemy team flag.

// On death: save the build
var echo = new EchoSave(character, rites, survivedSeconds);
using var f = FileAccess.Open("user://echo.json", FileAccess.ModeFlags.Write);
f.StoreString(JsonSerializer.Serialize(echo));

Wave 8Starter kit audit

DarkRewar's Survivors Starter Kit. MIT licence, about 1,100 lines of C#, last updated April 2026. Here's what you keep, fix, and add.

PartStatusWhat it means for you
Player movement + cameraKeepCharacterBody3D with WASD and arrow keys. Ready to use.
4 spellsKeepThey become the first 4 rite effects.
5 enemies + skeleton artKeepMinion, Warrior, Archer, Mage, Boss. KayKit and Kenney assets included.
Paired upgrade cardsKeepPlayer buff + enemy buff on every card. This is the base for The Crypt Learns.
Upgrades as .tres ResourcesKeepData-driven start. Extend it to rites.
200-enemy limitFixThe README says FPS drops past 200. Target 500.
BulletsFixA new RigidBody3D is created every shot. Pool them.
Nearest enemy searchFixSorts every enemy on every shot. Use a spatial grid.
Enemy updatesFixEach enemy runs its own process, LookAt, and timer. Move them into one manager loop.
Upgrade codeFixA big enum switch. Adding one spell means editing 3 files. Replace with RiteDefinition.
Hard-coded node pathsFixPaths like /root/MainScene/HUD break when you add a menu. Use signals.
Twitch vote leftoversFixA 3-second delay after each pick and username labels. Remove them.
XP on level-upFixExtra XP resets to 0. Carry it over.
TextFixUpgrade names are in French. Translate to English.
Death + game overAddNone in the kit. HP hits 0 and play continues.
XP gems, dash, reroll, banishAddXP is given right on kill. No gems, no dash, no reroll.
Boss timingAddBosses only spawn from a random enemy card or the B debug key. Add a schedule.
Bomb enemyAddThe EnemyBomb type exists but does nothing yet.

BossBuild plan

Part-time pace. Each phase ends with a check you can play.

WhenPhaseWorkDone when
Day 1Set upInstall Godot 4.6 .NET. Fork the kit to your GitHub. Open it twice, since the README says the first open can crash. Translate the text.The kit runs and you can level up.
Wk 1–2Make it fastPool bullets. Move enemies from one manager loop. Add a spatial grid for nearest enemy and hits. Cut skeleton animation for far enemies.500 enemies at 60 FPS, using the kit's stress-test spawn line.
Wk 3Make it data-drivenAdd RiteDefinition. Auto-load every .tres in a folder. Replace the enum switch. Swap node paths for signals.A new rite needs only a new .tres file.
Wk 4–5Core loopDeath and game over. XP gems with magnet and merge. Carry-over XP. Reroll and banish. Dash. Bosses at 3, 6, 12 minutes, final at 15.A full 15-minute run you can win or lose.
Wk 6–7RitesTrigger system. 4 kit spells become effects. Build 8 rites. Add tag set bonuses.Two runs with different rites play differently.
Wk 8The Crypt LearnsDamage-by-tag tracker. Counter-pick enemy card. 50% resist cap. Card shows both sides.A one-tag build feels pressure by minute 10.
Wk 9Your EchoSave build on death. Echo boss at 9:00. Reward: keep one of its rites.You fight your last build and recognize it.
Wk 10–12Content + polish3 characters, 12 items, charger and bomber with floor warnings, effects slider, debug spawner.5 friends play it. Track where they quit.

The kit uses C#, so heavy enemy logic already runs faster than GDScript. Halls of Torment moved its hot paths to C++ for the same reason.

Your call

The kit's dungeon art pushes Echo Crypt toward dark loot like Halls of Torment. Do you want to keep that look, or swap the art for something else before you build content?
